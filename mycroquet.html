myCroquet

41 clubs · all active members · live now

myCroquet is Queensland croquet's member platform, built on a simple premise: the platform itself is small, and everything else is a module. The permanent core does almost nothing — sign in, see and edit your own details. Every other feature is a self-contained module, switched on per person, visible only to the people it serves.

Each person's myCroquet is exactly as large as their responsibilities, and no larger. A member sees a quiet little site. A club secretary sees membership tools. A committee member sees the decision register. Nobody scrolls past functions they never asked for.

What it does

  • Passwordless login — no account setup required for members
  • Modular by design — every feature is a module a person either sees or doesn't
  • Handicap module — GC and AC ratings, fully indexed
  • Come & Try module — tracks every prospect from sign-up to membership
  • Club admin — secretaries manage their own records without IT support
  • Open standard — anyone, human or AI, can build a module that plugs in; it arrives switched off and goes live only after review

New features are installed invisible, shown first to a handful of people, then widened — so the platform gains capability indefinitely without ever growing harder to use. The full thinking is in the essay: myCroquet: A Modular Platform.
