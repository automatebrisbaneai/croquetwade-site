How this calendar was put together — and how clashes were resolved

What this is. One 2027 calendar for all four codes. The Association Croquet dates are the AC committee's, supplied by Willy Silk. Golf Croquet, Ricochet and Gateball follow the events coordinator's proposed schedule. The national fixtures are set, arranged around the AC Nationals coming to Brisbane in March.

How the dates were chosen. The AC committee submitted their dates as a firm requirement — they asked that these exact dates be set for the AC calendar. To meet that, the AC dates were locked in as sent and every other event, across all four codes, was worked in around them.

How clashes were resolved. Every date was checked against every other, against the national fixtures, and against the big club competitions. Where the two lists put the same event on different dates, the AC committee's date was used (the five listed below). Two of those choices also prevent a double-booking: keeping the May date for the Division 2 Medal would have collided with the committee's new Alternate Strokes Doubles on the same weekend, and keeping the late-October date for the Division 2 Championship would have collided with the Bronze Medal. A few events deliberately share a weekend — Men's and Women's Open Singles, the Division 3 and 4 Medals, and the Division 1 and 2 Championships — because they run as pairs, so those aren't clashes. Nothing lands on the major club draws (MSOB on the Broadwater, Stephens Easter, the Redcliffe carnival), and no medal or championship falls on a public-holiday long weekend — the one exception being GC Men's Open Singles, which deliberately uses the Labour Day long weekend as a four-day event.

Venues. Most 2027 host clubs aren't allocated yet, so all but a few venues read TBC. The box below lists everything still to confirm.
