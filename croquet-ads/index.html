Our online advertising is live

Croquet Queensland has started showing the banners below around the web, to people who have already visited our news site or our main site and are here in Australia. They are mostly our own members and recent visitors. So this is less a hard advertisement and more a friendly reminder, the small lift you get from seeing your sport turn up on television. The point is for croquet to feel present, and for people to feel a little more energised and positive about the game. It runs for about $50 a month and is measured by how many people see croquet, not by how many click.
